The daily liturgy

The 20-Minute Sanctuary

Set aside a little time each day, just fifteen to twenty minutes, to let your attention settle. A small, steady reading ritual can become an oasis of contemplation in a busy week. There is no finish line—only the quiet pleasure of returning to a page.

“I read in order to explore new worlds, both real and imagined, and return to our own lives with an altered, more compassionate gaze.”
